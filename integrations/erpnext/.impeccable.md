## Design Context

### Users
ERPNext users completing business queries and document tasks through the embedded Zebra assistant.

### Brand Personality
Calm, clear, dependable. The user explicitly selected Trench's light conversation UI as the reference and requested reuse of its sidebar and composer.

### Aesthetic Direction
Preserve the approved Trench layout, Zebra branding, green accent, restrained borders and spacious fullscreen chat. This change adds GSAP motion without changing information hierarchy.

### Design Principles
- Motion explains panel, conversation and execution-state changes.
- Keep controls immediately usable; no bouncing or constant decoration.
- Reveal a new message once; never replay its entrance for each streamed token.
- Scope motion to Agent surfaces and their dialogs, preserving ERPNext business screens.
- Honor reduced motion, touch input and cleanup when surfaces disappear.
